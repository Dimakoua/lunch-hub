King Street West is Toronto's hub for tech offices, creative agencies, and condo dwellers—which means the midday lunch rush is serious business. Whether you have 20 minutes between standups or you're booking a table for a client lunch, King West has a massive range of options.

Here are the best lunch spots on King West in Toronto right now, organized by vibe and budget.

## 1. Wilbur Mexicana (554 King St. W) — Best Fast-Casual Tacos
If you want bold flavours without sacrificing your entire lunch hour, **Wilbur Mexicana** is a King West institution. Order at the counter, grab a seat, and hit the self-serve salsa bar (which ranges from mild tomatillo to fiery habanero).
- **What to order:** Baja Fish Tacos, Steak Quesadilla, or the Pork Belly Burrito.
- **Price range:** \$12–\$18
- **Best for:** Solo lunches and quick team outings.

## 2. Gusto 101 (101 Portland St.) — Best for Client Lunches
Just steps off King on Portland Street, **Gusto 101** is housed in a converted auto garage with a lively, sun-drenched dining room and one of the best year-round rooftop patios in the city. Their weekday lunch service hits the sweet spot between polished and relaxed.
- **What to order:** Mafalde ai Funghi (wild mushroom pasta) or the Cavolo Nero salad with grilled chicken.
- **Price range:** \$22–\$35
- **Best for:** Impressing clients or celebrating a team milestone.

## 3. Pai Northern Thai Kitchen (18 Duncan St.) — Best Comfort Lunch
Technically a block east of Spadina in the Entertainment District, **Pai** is an easy walk from anywhere on King West and remains one of Toronto's most beloved Thai restaurants. It gets busy at noon, so arriving at 11:45 AM or ordering ahead is a smart move.
- **What to order:** Khao Soi (coconut curry noodle soup) or Pad Gra Prow.
- **Price range:** \$18–\$25
- **Best for:** Cold Toronto days when you need a warming, flavour-packed meal.

## 4. Banh Mi Boys (392 Queen St. W) — Best Under-$15 Walk
A five-minute walk north of King West, **Banh Mi Boys** is unbeatable for a fast, affordable, and filling lunch. Their crispy baguettes and steamed bao buns are packed with pickled veggies, herbs, and your choice of protein.
- **What to order:** Five Spice Pork Belly Banh Mi or Fried Chicken Bao.
- **Price range:** \$9–\$15
- **Best for:** Budget-friendly lunches on the go.
